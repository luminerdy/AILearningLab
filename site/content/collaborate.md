# COLLABORATE: Improve Your Project and Teaching Lab

Use focused conversation with AI to investigate a difficulty, compare possible changes, and improve a small project and the lab that teaches it.

**I can collaborate with AI to make a checked improvement—and explain why it helped.**

Start here if you want practice improving a result through conversation and evidence. You do not need to complete ASK first. Bring a small project you can run, use an instructor-prepared starter, or reuse a project from another workshop. If you have no teaching lab yet, begin with simple run instructions and create the lab in Lab 3. Software-only projects are a complete path. Cameras, Pico, breadboarding, and robots are optional contexts.

## Choose your starting point

- **New to AI or coding:** Use a tested starter and its run instructions. Ask AI to explain its purpose, predict one result, and run it before choosing an improvement. Your instructor can help with the terminal and checkpoints.
- **Have a small working project:** Bring it and any existing instructions. Use Lab 1 to find a worthwhile improvement.
- **Already comfortable iterating:** Use the same evidence requirements and choose a stretch challenge that helps you learn something new.

Choose ASK if your immediate need is describing an idea and creating a first version. Choose COLLABORATE if your immediate need is discussing, investigating, and improving a result. Move between practices as useful.

## Keep the project small

Choose one learning goal, one bounded change at a time, and three observable checks. A three-question quiz, two-choice story, one-control simulation, or single-page learning tool is enough. For a bigger ambition, ask: **What is one small behavior I can build, test, and teach today?**

Use your prepared Raspberry Pi 500 and Codex CLI project folder. Work individually; discuss freely and seek help. Peer feedback is optional. Save your own decisions and evidence.

## Follow the labs

| Lab | What you practice | What you save |
|---|---|---|
| [1: Notice and Compare](https://luminerdy.github.io/AILearningLab/collaborate-1.html) | Describe evidence, ask questions, compare options | Baseline, finding, bounded brief, chosen approach |
| [2: Change and Check](https://luminerdy.github.io/AILearningLab/collaborate-2.html) | Direct a focused change, inspect, test, redirect | Before/after version and repeated checks |
| [3: Improve and Teach](https://luminerdy.github.io/AILearningLab/collaborate-3.html) | Revise the lab from actual behavior and learner needs | Revised instructions, trial, reflection |

Plan two two-hour sessions as a first pilot estimate, including breaks. Adjust for access, project complexity, and hardware. This is practice in COLLABORATE, not a rank or a prerequisite for later practices.

Use the [complete workbook](collaborate-workbook.md) to save your work and the [instructor guide](collaborate-instructors.md) to teach the sessions.
